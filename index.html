About Me

My name is Jørgen Maurstad Leiros and I am currently at the end of an exciting academic journey where I've had the pleasure of studying how technology has transformed businesses and society. As early as middle school, I was sure that I would get a degree in economics and business, inspired by members of my extended family who had successful careers in the field. However, through coincidence, luck and determination, I have been able to explore economics, business administration, information technology, and data science. This journey has given me a wealth of experiences, broad knowledge, and it has made me confident that I am in the right field.
