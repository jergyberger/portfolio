Project Overview


During my first semester at the Norwegian University of Science and Technology, three peers and I developed Blackout Escape Room as part of our web development exam. The game begins with the player waking up in a pitch-black room after a party, tasked with finding their belongings and solving puzzles to escape through various rooms and get home. It was an exciting project that blended creativity, storytelling, and technical skills.
