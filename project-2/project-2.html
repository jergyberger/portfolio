Airbnb Dashboard

Project Overview

In this project, my primary focus was to design and develop a dashboard that provides actionable insights into Airbnb's performance on two key strategic priorities: (1) making hosting mainstream and (2) perfecting the core service. The goal was to evaluate adoption rates among hosts and measure customer satisfaction and operational efficiency, with a particular emphasis on pricing dynamics.

While this was a team effort, I was primarily responsible for the database design and query optimization. Additionally, I contributed to data cleaning and visualization, ensuring that the insights were both accurate and effectively communicated. This project marked my first experience with designing a dashboard and it allowed me to combine technical skills with business objectives, delivering a product that aligned with Airbnb’s strategic goals.
