Dry Bulk Premium Prediction - Deep Neural Network

Project Overview


In my master thesis, I am exploring how deep learning models can be applied to predict dry bulk freight rates for Handysize vessels. This research also investigates the potential of integrating diverse data sources, including economic indicators and geopolitical events, to enhance predictions. While I am deeply invested in the project and excited about its potential applications, the details remain confidential at this time. This work combines my passion for data science with its real-world implications in the maritime industry.
