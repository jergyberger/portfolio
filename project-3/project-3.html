NYC Traffic Safety - Data Engineering and Dashboard

Project Overview


This project focused on analyzing traffic incident data in New York City to uncover actionable insights aimed at reducing crashes and fatalities. Using the CRISP-DM framework, we collected, cleaned, and structured a large dataset of traffic collisions reported by the NYPD, integrating it with weather and geospatial data for a comprehensive analysis. The primary objective was to identify key contributing factors to severe accidents and present these insights through an interactive dashboard designed for the New York City Department of Transportation.

My role in the project involved data engineering and cleaning, where I led efforts to preprocess and transform the dataset to ensure high data quality and readiness for analysis. I designed and implemented the SQL database structure, following a star schema approach to optimize querying and support visualization tasks. Additionally, I contributed to creating visualizations that highlighted trends, contributing factors, and the impact of weather on traffic safety, enabling executives to make data-driven decisions on policies and interventions.

The final dashboard offers insights into accident hotspots, high-risk vehicle types, and weather-related risks, supporting better planning and resource allocation to improve road safety across New York City.
